What is new since the last build:
- Play together: whoever grabs a card first keeps it, the other player's card slides back with a short note, and a drag never gets stuck. Invite links now show the real picture and board in the message preview and open the app directly.
- New board sizes: Triangles 3x3 and Hexagons 4x4 and 6x6. A few edge pieces of a unique shape start in place, so the picture stays a full rectangle. Dark night cards that differ only by a star or two count as the same card.
- Fixes from your feedback: the whole picture always shows before the deal, also after Next puzzle; the daily gift counts on past day 7 (Day 8 to 14 in the second week); daily packs of 2023 and 2024 have their month names again; the Home Screen widget shows today's picture instead of a grey square.
- Gentler haptics: no strong buzzes, a soft run of ticks as cards are dealt or shuffled, and a success tap when a picture is complete.
