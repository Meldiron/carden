Thank you for reviewing Carden.

ACCOUNTS
No login is needed: the first launch creates an anonymous guest on our backend (Appwrite, EU). Sign in with Apple and email are optional and keep the same progress (Profile > Settings > Account). Delete account: Profile > Settings > Manage account > Delete account.

GUIDED START
New players go from the welcome pages into a first 9-card puzzle, then a simple Home; the rest opens as pictures are solved (Library 3, Quests 20, Social 50 and others). To open everything: Profile > Settings > Guided start off, or "Unlock anyway" on Home. The Store is always open from the coin balance.

PLAY TOGETHER
A live shared board for up to 5 people, who join by an invite link. Device A: open a picture, start the board, tap Pause > Play together; the share sheet opens with a link (join.carden.page/...). Open it on device B: the app if installed (universal link), else Safari (the web version, no account needed; a Mac works too). Both move cards on the same board, each player in their own colour. The players button at the top lists everyone: the host can Remove a player or End game for everyone, a guest can Leave game. Only people with the link can join: no matchmaking, no strangers, no chat (only card moves are shared). A player's page has Report and Block; blocked players cannot join each other's games. My Puzzles photos cannot be shared.

FRIEND GIFTS
Friends are added by friend code (Social > Friends). On a friend's page, Send a gift sends 1 ticket or 150 coins, free for the sender, once a day per friend; it arrives in the friend's Social > Inbox and expires after 24 hours. Gifts cannot be bought or traded. Gift notifications and messages from Carden are push notifications.

PURCHASES
StoreKit 2 consumables (coins, tickets, booster bundles of Peeks, Hints and Magic) and two auto-renewable subscriptions in the "VIP" group (vip_monthly, vip_yearly), both with a 7-day free trial and Family Sharing. Nothing is granted on the device: our server verifies Apple's signed transaction (JWS) and grants once; Sandbox purchases verify too. To test: tap the coin balance (Store) and buy with a Sandbox Apple ID. Restore purchases and Redeem a gift code (Apple offer codes) are in Profile > Settings > Account.

LUCKY WHEEL
A spin costs 1 ticket and pays one random prize: coins, Peeks, Hints or Magic (×5 spin: 5 tickets, the prize paid five times, same odds). Tickets can be bought, so the age rating declares a loot box. "See odds" lists every prize and its chance before a spin. Tickets are never a prize.

ADS AND ANALYTICS
No ad network and no third-party SDK for ads, analytics or tracking. Break cards and the "rewarded video" are closable house ads for the game's own store. Usage analytics go only to our own backend (Appwrite Analytics), are not used for tracking, and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy > Share usage data.

WIDGET
The Home Screen widget (small, medium, large) shows today's daily picture; a tap opens that puzzle.

REMINDERS
After the second picture the app asks once: a switch per kind (daily and event reminders, friend gifts, messages from Carden), then Allow shows the iOS prompt. Each has its own switch in Settings. Shaking the iPhone opens Send feedback (off in Settings > Help).

CONTENT
Catalogue pictures are public-domain or openly licensed (Wikimedia Commons, Openverse, Flickr, Pixabay, NASA, museums); some are AI-generated or made by us. Each shows its author and licence.

PRIVATE PACKS
A few free gift packs open with a code redeemed only on the web version; the iPhone app has no code entry. Nothing about them is sold.

SOCIAL
Player pages show names, avatars and statistics. There is no chat; the display name is the only text players share, checked against a list of offensive words. Social > Inbox shows our messages, which cannot be answered. On any player's page, Report sends a report we read and Block hides the player; Settings > Blocked players unblocks.
