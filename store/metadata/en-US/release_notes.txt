Version 2.0.0

Card Jigsaw World is now Carden, your calm picture puzzle, with a new icon: a lotus in coloured glass. Your progress, coins and packs stay as they are.

Play together
- Play together in a board's pause menu shares a link. Up to five people play the same picture live, in the app or in a browser, each in their own colour. The host can remove a player or end the game, and picks the next picture for everyone.
- Friend gifts: once a day, send each friend 1 ticket or 150 coins from their page. It waits in their Inbox for 24 hours, with a notification on iPhone if they allow it.

A calmer start
- New players begin with a first picture right after the welcome, and a simple Home. The Library, daily puzzle, Quests and the rest open as you solve pictures, or right away with Unlock anyway.
- Social > Inbox: messages from Carden, sometimes with a gift.

New ways to play
- Square boards: as many cards across as down, from 3x3 to 7x7. Expand cuts the whole picture in its own shape instead.
- Cards that look the same count as placed in either place.
- Painting: cards start in black and white and get their colour back as they lock. Pays 25% more.
- Giant boards of hundreds of cards, with pinch to zoom, an overview map that shows where a tapped card belongs, Gather with Undo, and Giant packs of detailed pictures made for them.
- Shuffle (free) replaces Undo, Magic places a card and its neighbours, Peek (10 coins) flies every card to its place for a moment, and a group you push moves aside whole.
- Squares, Triangles, Hexagons, Painting and giant boards open as you level up, each with a short intro.

Quests and rewards
- Quests that do not overlap, each asking for something different. Swap a quest you do not fancy for 1 ticket, and Claim all collects every reward.
- Lucky Wheel: a ×5 spin for 5 tickets pays its prize five times. Some prizes are Peeks and Magic, and See odds lists every prize and its chance.
- The piggy bank grows to 1,000 coins with upgrades, and booster bundles in the Store hold Peeks, Hints and Magic.
- Level-ups pay up to 300 coins, 500 every 10th level. VIP members get their 500 bonus coins automatically each week, and VIP can be shared through Family Sharing.

Home and Library
- Every new picture opens on the mode, board size and Painting you chose last; Quick play too.
- Once today's daily puzzle is solved, Home shows tomorrow's as a blurred preview.
- Library search finds pictures by title, new packs carry a New badge, and there are Finished, New and Hide finished filters.

Settings and account
- Notifications with a switch for each: daily reminders at your time, a note before a world-day event if you have been away, friend gifts and messages from Carden.
- Shake your iPhone on any screen to send us feedback.
- Settings > Accessibility: text size up to 200%, Reduce motion, Increase contrast and Differentiate without colour.
- Settings > Privacy > Share usage data: usage data goes only to our own servers, and you can turn it off.
- Delete your account in the app: Profile > Settings > Manage account > Delete account.
- Report or block a player from their page; blocked players leave your leaderboard and cannot join your games. Player names are checked for offensive words.
- Links to play.carden.page open the matching screen in the app. Dark and tinted Home Screen icons on iOS 18.

Pictures and sync
- A rebuilt catalogue served from our own servers: no people, no repeats, at least 20 pictures per pack, new Illustration, AI and Abstract packs.
- One save on every device: progress from the iPhone and the web adds up, play made offline is kept, and records only improve.
- One leaderboard, ranked by total XP; it and My Puzzles load more as you scroll. My Puzzles photos are private to your account; free players keep up to 5.
- Finishing a puzzle pays fewer coins. Six starter packs, one per category, are free; the other library packs cost coins, and any pack you have already played stays open.
